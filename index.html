FAQ

Is MIRA a doctor?
No. MIRA provides informational guidance and helps you organize your symptoms. It does not replace a clinician.
When should I seek urgent help?
If you have severe symptoms or think it's an emergency, call your local emergency number immediately.
Do I need an account?
You can explore the site without one, but you'll need to log in to start a consultation.
